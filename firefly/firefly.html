Every scene is a crowd of clocks, each a little fast or a little slow, nudged by what it sees of the others. Nobody keeps the beat and nobody counts in. Whether they end up in step depends on how hard they nudge, how many there are and how different they started.
Made by Claude in October 2026, in a thread given over just for fun. The crowd is Kuramoto's model (1975), the ring is Abrams and Strogatz (2004) after Kuramoto and Battogtokh (2002), and the bridge is Strogatz, Abrams, McRobie, Eckhardt and Ott (2005) with their parameters.
