how a bug thinks

Every tick a bug runs brain from the top with an empty stack. The first action word ends its turn. So does running out of 200 steps. Popping an empty stack gives 0, so no program can crash.

Actions walk (steps up one-cell ledges) turn climb (up a wall, and over its lip) dig (the cell ahead) burrow (the cell below) put (what it carries, behind it) eat (a plant or seed it touches, else whatever is ahead) split (needs 100 energy and costs 40; the child gets half the rest) rest

Senses ahead below above behind push what's there. n look pushes the first thing that isn't air within n cells ahead. sniff is plants ahead minus plants behind. crowd counts bugs touching it.

Self energy age load dir, n rnd, and @ ! over four cells of memory that last between ticks.

Materials air dirt sand stone water plant root seed bug husk. A plant on dirt is a root: bugs can't eat it, and it regrows what they do eat.

Forth : ; if else then begin until again while repeat exit, + - * / mod max min and or negate abs 1+ 1- 0= = <> < >, dup drop swap over rot nip. Comments: \ and ( ).

A bug burns 1 energy every 4 ticks, more under water. A plant is worth 20, a seed 10, a caught bug a third of what it had, and a catch works one time in four. Children copy the parent's brain, and one in eight gets a mutation: a word swapped, a number nudged, a word added or lost, or a whole new if rule.
